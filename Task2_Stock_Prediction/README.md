# Task 2: Predict Future Stock Prices (Short-Term)

## Objective
The objective of this task is to predict the next day's stock closing price using historical stock market data obtained from Yahoo Finance. Machine Learning techniques are applied to analyze market trends and forecast future prices.

## Dataset Used
Source: Yahoo Finance (yFinance API)
Stock: Apple Inc. (AAPL)
Features:
. Open Price
. High Price
. Low Price
. Close Price
. Volume
Target:
. Next Day Closing Price

## Libraries Used
. Pandas
. NumPy
. yFinance
. Matplotlib
. Scikit-Learn
. Jupyter Notebook

### Data Collection
. Downloaded historical stock data using yFinance
. Saved dataset as CSV file
. Loaded data into a Pandas DataFrame

### Data Inspection
. Checked dataset shape
. Displayed column names
. Viewed first five rows
. Checked for missing values
. Generated statistical summary

### Feature Engineering
. Created a target variable using the next day's closing price
. Removed null values generated by shifting
. Selected relevant features for prediction

### Model Development
. Split dataset into training and testing sets
. Trained a Linear Regression model
. Generated predictions on test data

### Model Evaluation
. Mean Absolute Error (MAE)
. Mean Squared Error (MSE)
. Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE)
. R² Score

### Data Visualization
. Closing Price Trend Plot
. Actual vs Predicted Closing Price Plot

## Key Findings
. Historical stock prices can be used to predict future closing prices.
. Open, High, Low, and Volume significantly influence stock price movements.
. The Linear Regression model was able to capture the relationship between input features and future prices.
.Visualization of Actual vs Predicted prices demonstrated the model's prediction capability.

## Results
The stock price prediction model was successfully developed using historical market data. The Linear Regression algorithm produced predictions for future closing prices and was evaluated using multiple performance metrics. The project demonstrates the practical application of regression techniques in financial data analysis and forecasting.
